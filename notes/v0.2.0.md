Tendril at home on Windows: Windows 11's own design, a Settings window, an installer, and a new mark. Most of it was found by running Tendril on a real Windows desktop after every change.

- **Windows 11's design, on Windows.** The launcher and Settings use Windows' own greys, the accent colour you picked in Windows, Segoe UI, and lists and controls drawn the way Windows draws them. Settings gets Mica when Tendril draws with the GPU. The glow and the gradient line start off there; Appearance turns them back on.
- **A Settings window.** Tabs for General, Appearance, Layout, Search Engines, Quicklinks and About. Add and edit your search engines and quicklinks there; every change is saved at once. Open it with "Settings", Ctrl , or the tray icon. It sits in the taskbar like any other window and has its own rounded frame.
- **An installer for Windows.** Per user, no admin: Tendril goes in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs, with a Start menu entry, an entry in Settings → Apps to uninstall it, and an offer to start it when you sign in. Updates keep the version shown there in step.
- **A new mark.** The launcher as a layered object, in blue and white, on the app, the tray and the installer.
- **Pin your favourites.** Ctrl K → Add to Favourites keeps anything at the top before you type, in the order you pinned it. Favourites now fill whole rows of tiles.
- **Windows Settings pages by name.** Type "bluetooth", "display", "default apps" and about thirty more.
- **Run as Administrator,** in a Windows app's actions.
- **Opens on the screen your pointer is on,** not always the first.
- **What's new after an update:** the top of the list links to it.
- **Clicking away closes it on Windows and macOS.** A new settings file was written with this turned off by mistake, so Tendril stayed open, and the shortcut then closed it instead of opening it. It's turned back on for you once.
- **Recording a shortcut works on Windows.** Pressing Ctrl, Alt or Shift alone was taken as a whole shortcut before the real key arrived.
- **Opens at once and in front on Windows,** with an icon in the notification area as another way in. Apps are listed the moment Tendril starts, from last time, while it looks for new ones.
- **A log on Windows** at %LOCALAPPDATA%\tendril\tendril.log, for when something goes wrong. On Linux, `./install.sh --uninstall` takes Tendril away again.

Files, snippets and sums with units, and a clear look at what Tendril keeps about you.

- **Find files by name as you type.** Your Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music and Videos are looked through in the background, and matches show under Files. It works the moment Tendril starts, from last time's list, while it looks again. Index Files says how many it found and when.
- **Choose what's searched.** Settings has a Files tab: pick folders with the system's folder picker, keep only some kinds of file, and leave things out by name (like node_modules), by path, or by regular expression. Hidden files stay out, and a folder's .gitignore is followed, unless you say otherwise.
- **More for files.** Ctrl K on a file: Copy File to paste it into a folder or a chat, Copy Name, Open With… on Windows, and Move to Recycle Bin (or Trash), which asks first.
- **Snippets.** Text you paste by typing its name or keyword, with {date}, {time} and {clipboard} filled in. Add them on the new Snippets tab.
- **Unit conversions.** "5 km in miles", "72 f to c", "2 GB in MB", "90 min in hours": length, weight, volume, area, time, data, speed and temperature, worked out on your computer.
- **Privacy & Data in Settings.** How ranking works in plain words, everything Tendril keeps and where, with buttons to show or delete each, and the only two things that go online.
- **Sharp text at 125% and other scaling,** with blur on or off. Text was drawn between pixels and looked soft.
- **Sharper website icons, sooner.** Quicklinks' icons are kept at twice the size, an SVG where the site has one, and several sites are asked at once.
- **Settings can be resized,** and opens where you left it.
- **Backspace goes back** out of Appearance, Clipboard History or Switch Windows when nothing's typed, and what you typed is cleared when Tendril closes.

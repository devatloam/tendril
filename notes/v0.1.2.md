A Settings window, and fixes found by running Tendril on a real Windows desktop.

- **Settings, like Raycast's.** A window with tabs for General, Appearance, Layout, Search Engines, Quicklinks and About. Add and edit your search engines and quicklinks there; every change is saved at once. Open it with "Settings", Ctrl , or the tray icon. It has its own dark, rounded frame instead of the old system one, sits in the taskbar like any other window, and doesn't stay on top of everything.
- **Clicking away closes it on Windows and macOS.** A new settings file was being written with this turned off by mistake, so Tendril stayed open, and pressing the shortcut then closed it instead of opening it. It's turned back on for you once.
- **Recording a shortcut works on Windows.** Pressing Ctrl, Alt or Shift on its own was taken as a whole shortcut before the real key arrived.
- **Opens faster and takes focus on Windows.** The window is kept between uses instead of made afresh each time.
- **An icon in the notification area** opens Tendril on Windows; right-click it for Appearance and Quit. It's the way in when another app holds your shortcut.
- **Apps are listed the moment Tendril starts,** from last time, while it looks for new ones.
- **Useful commands first.** Before you type, Clipboard History, Switch Windows and Appearance come first; Lock, Sleep and Shut Down come last.
- **A log on Windows** at %LOCALAPPDATA%\tendril\tendril.log, for when something goes wrong.
